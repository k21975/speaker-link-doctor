Speaker Link Doctor

Troubleshoot a Sonos system: see how every speaker connects, how strong each link is, and what to fix.

What it needs

  • An iPhone on iOS 18 or later.
  • Sonos speakers on the same network, or reachable by their IP address.

No speakers to hand? Tap Try the demo on the start screen to explore a made-up home.

Help

If the app finds no speakers, check that it has local network access: Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, Speaker Link Doctor.

For anything else, email speakerlinkdoctor@gmail.com. If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app.

Privacy

The app sends nothing about your home to its developer. Read the privacy policy.
